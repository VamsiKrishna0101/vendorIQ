Document Title: AWS Enterprise Cloud Solutions Overview - Pricing, Architecture, Compliance & Customer Experience 2026
Source: Official AWS Whitepapers & Pricing Pages
AWS primarily uses a pay-as-you-go pricing model with additional options like Savings Plans (up to 72% discount for 1-3 year commitments), Reserved Instances, and Spot Instances. For a standard enterprise workload (1000 vCPU, 4TB storage, moderate data transfer, EKS cluster), Year 1 TCO is approximately $1.85M – $2.1M depending on commitment level. With 3-year Savings Plans, costs reduce to ~$1.42M in Year 2 and $1.38M in Year 3.
Enterprise Support adds 10% of monthly usage (minimum fees apply). Hidden costs include data egress ($0.08-$0.09/GB), support fees, and additional tooling for advanced networking.
Technical Architecture: Core services include EC2, EKS (Kubernetes), S3, RDS, Lambda, SageMaker. Strong auto-scaling and global infrastructure with 30+ regions. EKS requires additional configuration for advanced networking and observability. High scalability but can introduce technical debt with proprietary services.
Compliance & Legal: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, GDPR, FedRAMP. S3 SLA 99.99% with up to 25% service credits. Strong data residency options.
Market Position: Market leader with ~31% share. Major customers: Netflix, Capital One, Airbus, NASA. Extensive case studies across industries.
Customer Experience: Enterprise Support SLA <15 minutes for critical cases. Large migrations typically take 4-8 weeks. Mixed feedback on support quality at scale; some reports of slow response outside Premier tier.
Red Flags: High egress costs, potential bill shock from unoptimized resources, Enterprise Support fees not always transparent in initial quotes
