Document Title: Google Cloud Enterprise Infrastructure Overview & Economics 2026
GCP offers on-demand + Committed Use Discounts and Sustained Use Discounts. For comparable workload, Year 1 TCO ~$1.78M. With 3-year commitments: Year 1 ~$1.55M, Year 2 $1.32M, Year 3 $1.28M — often strongest long-term pricing.
Technical Architecture: Best-in-class GKE (Autopilot mode reduces management overhead), superior data analytics (BigQuery), TPUs/Vertex AI for ML. Strong open-source alignment, lower vendor lock-in. Excellent for analytics and AI workloads.
Compliance: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, strong GDPR. Regional clusters SLA 99.95%, Autopilot 99.95%. Good data residency controls.
Market Position: Fastest growing hyperscaler. Strong in tech, media, gaming, and analytics. Customers: Spotify, PayPal, HSBC, Target, Airbnb.
Customer Experience: Premier Support <15 min for P1. Onboarding can take 6-10 weeks for complex enterprise setups. Technical support highly regarded, but enterprise sales/support sometimes slower.
Red Flags: Smaller enterprise footprint in traditional industries. Some gaps in legacy system integration compared to AWS/Azure
